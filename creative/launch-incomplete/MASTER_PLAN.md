# ASTAR launch production plan

Created 2 November 2026, Asia/Kolkata. The five days below are the proposed production schedule, not a claim that five days have elapsed. Digital deliverables may be prepared ahead of schedule; physical engineering and student research remain separate work.

| Day | Planned work | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — 2 Nov | Audit, canonical specification, references, brand foundation, preserve software | Audit and decisions; original files untouched; editable sources |
| 2 — 3 Nov | Reader interactions, Forge, product views and architecture | Executed tests; source-linked drawings; labelled concepts |
| 3 — 4 Nov | Actual software captures, 14-slide deck | Every slide inspected; editable PPTX and PDF |
| 4 — 5 Nov | 40-second film, poster, stickers, six social topics | Playable film; production package; exported graphics |
| 5 — 6 Nov | Packaging, student guide, brand manual, integrated QA and packaging | Checked files, linked launch index, truthful status |

## Work streams and dependencies

1. Software retains the Python preparation server and shared plain JavaScript reader. The launch copy is a reproducible delivery snapshot; the original project is preserved.
2. Product and brand establish a shared vocabulary. The V0 build guide governs hardware; the previous large-screen solar render is a future industrial concept.
3. Presentation and film consume actual reader/Forge captures, product drawings and the claim register.
4. Supporting print and social materials use the same identity and status labels.
5. Integration verifies navigation, persistence, grading, offline use, document layout, asset coverage and delivery links.

## Changes to the suggested sequence

- Existing work is substantial. Reuse and improve it instead of rebuilding unrelated apps.
- Software, product design and presentation can proceed in parallel after the audit.
- No public launch, outreach, manufacturing, hardware purchase or deployment is implied by producing this local launch package.
- Runtime measurements, battery/solar design, firmware integration, learner pilots and partnerships remain future work and cannot be completed by producing graphics.

## Release gates

- Do not describe a software interaction as tested until it has run successfully.
- Preserve original textbook wording and source placement. The distributable demonstration uses clearly identified original educational material; it does not masquerade as a published textbook.
- Automatic extraction warnings are diagnostic, not educational approval.
- Current V0 hardware is USB-powered. Solar operation is a later, untested concept.
- Deliver a useful intermediate for any unavailable export; record the exact limitation.
