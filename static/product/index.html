DESIGN BASIS

A concept you can take apart.

This model follows the current 4.2-inch, three-button solar brief. It does not describe the earlier seven-button, USB-only prototype.

Measured reference, estimated integration

The Waveshare SKU 13353 reference specifies a 400 × 300 display, an 84.8 × 63.6 mm active area and a 103 × 78.5 mm driver board. These dimensions are used here. Other board envelopes, mounting features and component placements are reasonable concept estimates.

The 124 × 90 mm body has a 6.03-inch diagonal; its 24 mm depth accommodates the layered concept. A nominal 5 V / 1 W rear panel and 3.7 V / 480 mAh cell are design targets, not tested output or runtime claims.

What still needs engineering

Confirm exact board revisions and connectors, enclosure tolerances, battery clearances, thermal behavior, solar charge regulation, load sharing, current limits and the full electrical schematic. Wiring in this model explains relationships; it is not a pin-level build guide.

USB data and USB charging are separate concept ports. Charge control, protection, power-path and regulation are separate functional elements on a proposed power board, not a verified purchasable module set.
