     the sum ‘0’ i.e. the additive identity. Any two numbers whose sum is ‘0’ are called the additive
     inverses of each other. In general if a represents any rational number then a + (−a) = 0 and (−
     a) + a = 0.

    Then ‘a’, ‘−a’ are additive inverse of each other.
